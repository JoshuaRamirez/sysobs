# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith and is smarter than
their worst message.

## What is expected

- Argue with the idea, not the person.
- Accept that "I was wrong" is a normal thing to say and costs nothing.
- Give people the context they need instead of the answer you find obvious.
- Remember that most contributors are doing this in stolen time.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, PRs or anywhere else
  the project touches.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information — including anything you happened to
  read out of a `sysobs` store — without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, and whenever you are representing the
project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, or by
opening an issue if the matter is not sensitive. Reports are handled by the
maintainer, kept confidential, and answered.

Consequences scale with the behaviour: a private word, a public correction, a
temporary block, or a permanent ban. The maintainer may remove or edit
comments, commits, issues and PRs that conflict with this document.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
