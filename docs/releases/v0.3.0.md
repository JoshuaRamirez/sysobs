# sysobs v0.3.0 — 2026-10-20

It keeps sampling under heavy load. Baseline `ca90e92` (v0.2.0).

His words, 10-20 15:47: "it should probably dynamically give itself CPU
priority when the system is pegged. It shouldn't crap out. It's necessary that
it keeps going. It has to take priority."

## What went wrong on 10-20, 13:45–14:00 local (load ~140 on 16 cores)

- The snapshot job ran at `Nice 10` and `ProcessType Background`. At load 140
  its `ps` and `top` timed out: the 21:09Z snapshot recorded 0 processes
  ("degraded: process").
- Each full snapshot rewrites the dimension tables whole (argv alone is
  822 MB) and folds in ~45k procwatch events; at that load one run took ~40
  minutes, and launchd does not start an interval job while it still runs. No
  snapshot landed between 20:29Z and 21:09Z.
- A second sysobs that met the store lock waited 120 s and died with
  `TimeoutError`.
- The SQLite mirror stopped at 2026-09-21: its sync reads the whole store
  into memory. procwatch itself was running and its events were in the CSVs.

## What changed

- A light sample (one `ps`) every tick, written at once outside the store:
  `sysobs light`.
- `watch` is the sampler: the full snapshot runs in a child, never two at once.
- A lock held means skip, not wait.
- Priority under load, the narrator's mechanism (auto-speech ADR-019); the
  full snapshot in background while calm, raised when pegged.
- The sampler and procwatch start at `ProcessType Interactive` (base 31, not
  launchd's default 20).
- The sampler and procwatch run under substrate, with an absolute interpreter.
